It is quick and straightforward:

  • YouTube: Open your playlist page, click Share, and click Copy Link (or copy the URL directly from your browser's address bar).
  • Spotify: Click the three dots (•••) on any playlist or song → select Share → click Copy link to playlist.

Bitrate affects audio fidelity and file size:

  • 320 kbps (Recommended): Highest studio-grade clarity with full dynamic range and punchy bass.
  • 256 kbps & 192 kbps: High-standard balanced quality with smaller file sizes.
  • 128 kbps: Compact file size, suitable if device storage is limited.

When downloading multiple items, the server bundles them into a single .ZIP archive with zero compression overhead for instantaneous download. Single tracks are saved directly in their native format (e.g. song.mp3 or video.mp4).

To ensure high performance and stability for all users, batches are capped at 200 tracks per session. Larger playlists can easily be split into multiple downloads.

100% Free and Safe. PlaylistGet does not serve malware, popups, or ads. All audio and video streams are encoded using official open-source FFmpeg and yt-dlp engines.
